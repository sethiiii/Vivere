# Vivere Privacy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Vivere is designed to work without an account or remote service.

## Data collection

Vivere does not collect, sell, rent, transmit, or use personal data for advertising or tracking. The app contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, telemetry client, hosted database, or AI/bot integration.

## Data stored on your devices

Habits, completions, settings, and Journal entries are stored locally using Apple’s Core Data framework. Photos are added only when you choose one and are stored with the local Journal entry.

If you enable the optional widget, Vivere places a compact snapshot in an Apple App Group container on the same device. If you install the Apple Watch companion, the iPhone and Watch exchange habit snapshots and completion requests through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework.

## Backups and sharing

Vivere exports a backup only when you request one. You choose its destination using Apple’s system document picker. Quote sharing also occurs only when you invoke the system share sheet and choose a destination.

## Notifications and permissions

Reminders are scheduled locally through Apple’s notification framework. Photo access is requested through Apple’s system photo picker only when you choose to attach a photo. Vivere does not request location, contacts, microphone, health, advertising, or cross-app tracking access.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual habits and Journal entries in the app. Removing Vivere from a device deletes its local app data, subject to any device backups you control.

## Contact

A dedicated support contact will be added before public App Store distribution. No personal email address is embedded in the app or this policy.
